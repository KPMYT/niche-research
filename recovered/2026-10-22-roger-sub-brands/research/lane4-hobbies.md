# Lane 4: Hobbies and off-the-clock interests (Roger sub-brands)

Researched 2026-10-22 with NexLev: search_niche_finder_channels (about 20 semantic queries), faceless_outliers_videos (3 queries, outlier ≥3x, long-form), youtube_channel_outliers (3 channels), search_viral_videos_small_channels (4 queries) and youtube_search (1 query).

**How to read the numbers**
- **Subs / $/mo / RPM** are NexLev channel stats. RPM is NexLev's `rpm.total` estimate in USD.
- **Outlier "x"** is a video's views divided by its channel's average.
- **Dates:** NexLev's `lastUploadedVideos` dates are month-approximate. They tend to land on the 19th, 20th or 22nd. Treat video ages as "about N months".
- **Monthly revenue** is NexLev's AdSense estimate. A channel can show $0 when it is not monetized yet.

## Key findings

1. **The veteran-persona + book model is already working in hobby niches.**
   - **James J. Conner** (@jamesjconner, BBQ) joined 2026-08-28. He has 8.26K subs, 19 videos, 1.09M views and an estimated $3,913/mo, with outlier score 4.55.
   - Every title carries "After 60 Years on the Pit".
   - He sells "The Highway 80 Pit Book".
   - His About text says: "The man you see and hear is generated with AI."
   - This is the operator's exact model, transplanted into BBQ.
2. **Roger's own title levers transfer into hobby niches almost word for word.**
   - "1 Cup of This Kills Every Weed — But Never Touches Your Grass" (Backyard Verdict, 297K)
   - "Hydrogen Peroxide Tricks Dealers Hope You NEVER Find Out" (MechaniCar, 125K, 200.8x)
   - "9 Things Gunsmiths Don't Want You to Know About Hydrogen Peroxide" (Wendell Coffey, 94K)
   - "The 3 Riding Mowers Mechanics Buy For Their Own Yards" (HardYardCo, 310K, 11.7x)
   - The last one is Roger's "what plumbers do in their own homes" lever.
3. **Brand-verdict and "Made in America" titles are the hottest pattern across tools, mowers, grills, knives and cast iron.** Examples: "The Only N ___ Still Worth Buying", "Only 3 ___ Still Made in America (X Isn't One)", "N ___ Brands Only DUMB People Buy". They pull 20x to 55x outliers on channels with fewer than 7K subs. **No channel delivers them from a named veteran tradesman.** That is Roger's gap.
4. **Car and mechanic secrets has the biggest demand but is crowded.** One search ("things mechanics never do to their own cars") returned at least 6 brand-new faceless persona channels whose videos have 9 to 293 views:
   - Warren's Garage
   - Frank Knows Cars
   - Klaus German Mechanic (sells a book)
   - Barry Mechanic (sells a book)
   - Hank Torren
   - Motor Archetype

   Others are already running the operator's play there.
5. **The highest RPM in this lane is backup power, outages and winter prep (about $5.5 to $6.7).** Brand-new channels are earning $4.5K/mo within weeks. Roger's audience tab already shows generators and home energy.
