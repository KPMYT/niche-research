- NexLev's "last uploaded videos" dates come in rounded (lots of the 19th and 20th), so video ages are given as approximate months.

## 0. The main finding (read this first)

1. **Culture-lens home channels are the fastest-growing format in Roger's audience right now.** Almost all of them are faceless AI-avatar "insider" personas: a Japanese mother, a Mexican mother, an Amish elder. Most launched between Mar and Oct 2026. Their top videos are Roger's own proven topics (hydrogen peroxide, drains, toilets, pests, "things X households never throw away") seen through a lens:
   - Bertha's Garden Hacks: "Hydrogen Peroxide Kills Roaches, Ants and Mice… (The Mexican Way)", **2.8M**.
   - Japanese Home Hacks: "16 Ways The Japanese Use Hydrogen Peroxide", **1.3M**.
   - Eli Yoder Secrets: "20 Hydrogen Peroxide Hacks That Kill Roaches…", **1.2M**.
   - Esther Yoder: "Throw it in the sink and the drain will never clog again! (The Amish Way)", **162K**.

   So the audience overlap is proven. These are Roger's titles with a passport stamped on them.
2. **The insider seat is saturated in Japanese, Mexican and Amish.** There are dozens of "Kenji / Maya / Emiko / Eli Yoder / Bertha" clones.
3. **The outsider seat is almost empty: an old American tradesman going to learn their way.** It is proven elsewhere, though:
   - Roger Wakefield's "JAPANESE Plumbing | American Plumber Reacts" has 382K views and "India Edition" has 956K (both about 5 years old).
   - fern, "The Strange Design of American Toilets": 1.1M.
   - Insider Tech, "Why US Bathrooms Don't Have Bidets": 4.8M.
   - Outsider-visits-the-Amish videos run 3–12M: Seek Discomfort 5.9M, BENOFTHEWEEK 4.7M, Peter Santenello 4.2M, Drew Binsky 12.7M, Discover Connection 3.4M.
   - Red Poppy Ranch, "Does Amish Air Conditioning Really Work? We're Going To Find Out! (Earth Tubes)": 1.2M.

   One tiny channel, **The Humbled Craftsman** (UCn5s7nNX2njWchS-OM2ER-Q), is trying exactly "American Plumber Sees a Japanese Toilet". Its two videos are 2–3 weeks old with 215 and 243 views. Nobody owns this seat yet.
4. **Roger's edge is the role.** He is the skeptical 45-year US plumber who has seen everything, being humbled (or not) by another culture's way. That gives every video a built-in verdict: "Does it actually work? Would I put it in my own house?" An insider persona can't deliver that.
5. **Conflicts to check with the operator:**
   - German is taken by Werner Finkbeiner.
   - plumbing-ideation already uses Mexican, Japanese and British "tradition keys" to re-make Roger/Werner titles.
   - The Navy-vet home-security persona overlaps with any "military way" idea.

   So a Roger lens channel has to stay **outsider / student / verdict** (not "(The X Way)" re-skins), and should not be German.
