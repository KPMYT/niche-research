# Lane 1: Channels like Silver Cymbal (multi-topic home-repair hosts)

Research date: 2026-10-22. Source: NexLev MCP (youtube_channel_outliers, youtube_search, search_niche_finder_channels). Every number below came from a tool result.

**About the ages.** NexLev's outlier tool rounds publish dates to "N years ago", so older ages are approximate (for example "~5 yrs"). Ages under one year are as YouTube shows them. "x" is NexLev's outlier ratio: views divided by the channel's average. RPM is NexLev's estimate: "base" is the niche RPM and "total" is the channel's blended RPM.
