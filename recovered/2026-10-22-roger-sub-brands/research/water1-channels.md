- **NexLev was unavailable for this whole run.** Every NexLev call (search_niche_finder_channels, check_faceless_channel, youtube_channel_about, youtube_search) returned `429 Too Many Requests` for about 45 minutes, across more than 10 retries. The parallel lanes have probably used up the quota.

Every other $/RPM/outlier cell is "n/a". A NexLev pass should fill those cells once the quota resets.

- "Joined" is the YouTube account date. Several faceless channels are **repurposed old accounts**, so for those the first on-topic upload is given as well.

## 2. Answers

### (1) How many channels own this niche, and how big are they?

**No single channel owns "home water" for US homeowners.** It splits into silos, each led by a different kind of channel:

- **Filter verdicts:** about 4 real testers. Project Farm (3.94M subs, one 2.06M water video, but generalist), Water Filter Guru (86.6K, the only full-time US water-filter reviewer, 940 videos), BOS Water (24.2K) and Modern Castle (37.9K). Water Filter Guru's new uploads get only 2-7K views, so the specialist is not growing.
- **Softeners/treatment:** 6-8 dealer and retailer channels (Water eStore 103K, Fresh Water Systems 31.4K, US Water Systems 23.1K, plus small dealers). Their big videos are 5-14 years old repair how-tos, and no recent softener video has broken out. The biggest dated softener hit this year is Matt Risinger at 84.5K (3w).
- **Well water:** H²O Mechanic (188K, face, repair vlogs) is the one large channel. Treatment content (iron, sulfur, bacteria) is dealers only. Their one outlier is SoftPro's 467K.
- **Bottled/tap "brands to avoid":** at least 8 faceless food-exposé channels (30K-211K subs, mostly launched 2025-26) run water as a recurring episode. The best is 1.85M (Everything Uncovered, 2mo).
- **PFAS/tap safety:** big science and media one-offs (Veritasium 37.2M). No specialist.
- **Emergency/off-grid:** large face prepper channels (City Prepping 1.27M, Dirty Civilian 835K) plus a faceless "suppressed science" swarm.

**Overall size:** water videos clear 1-2M when framed as a consumer exposé or a "cheap trick", and 100-300K when framed as advice. The advice specialists themselves are small to mid-size (25-190K subs).

### (2) Are there faceless channels, and how are they doing?

Yes, and they are the fastest growers. All are **general channels for which water is one strong episode**, not water channels:

- **Exposé listicles:**
  - Everything Uncovered: water test 1.85M in 2mo on a 30.7K channel.
  - Consumer Exposed: 163K subs since Jun 2026; water 462K.
  - Consumed, Dishclosure, Built to Eat, All Foods Uncovered, In Plain Bite: water episodes 168-488K.
- **Suppressed-science AI channels:**
  - Sidney Explains: water-from-air 2.43M.
  - Historia Prime: two-metals water 1.63M.
  - Wild Line: water machine 733K (1mo).
  - Plus at least 6 "copper + zinc purifies water" clones at 33K-663K.
- **Senior-health AI channels:** Senior Lane (created May 2026) "Never Drink Plain Water Again After 60" 1.0M in 2mo.
- **DIY-hands channels:** Holic Creation/Clever, with whole-house-filter and PVC filters at 1.3-3.2M.
- **Water-only faceless channels are tiny:** Forbidden Waters 2.08K subs, Water Clarity 158, SoftPro's city-SEO spam. **No faceless channel is a dedicated, successful home-water advice brand.**

### (3) Are there AI-avatar persona channels in water? Names and growth

Yes. Three are confirmed by in-video frames, plus one possible. All are under 3 months old on topic:

1. **Caleb Greer — Off-Grid Water & Power** (the presenter in the "GREER OFF-GRID" cap appears AI-generated in every frame, intercut with b-roll; this is my visual read).
   - Created **Jul 28, 2026**. Today: **18.1K subs, 1.46M views, 43 videos in about 12 weeks**. Earlier NexLev pull: 16.1K subs, ~$4.0K/mo, RPM 5.72.
   - Water hits: "2 Metals… Purify ANY Water" 395K (1mo) and "Make Your Own Water Filter…" 176K (2mo).
   - It has already drifted to heat and power ("$35 Aluminum Mesh" 258K in 4 days). It is an off-grid DIY persona, not a home-water advisor.
2. **The Off-Grid Well Finder** (a generated white-bearded "42-year well driller" at a workshop desk, intercut with reused drilling footage).
   - A repurposed account with **under 2 months on topic**: 934 subs, 250K views, 39 videos, uploading daily.
   - One breakout, "I Drilled Wells for 42 Years — 9 Things We Never Told Our Customers (#4 Saves $12,000)" at **161K in 3 weeks**. Everything else is 300 to 29K.
   - It is now posting "the only 3 [well pumps] I'd put in my own well" verdict titles. **This is the closest thing to Roger the Water Guy in the market**, but for well drilling and finding water, not water quality.
3. **My Simple Homestead** (AI Amish-elder persona): 7.75K subs; "FREE Water Forever! 11 Off-Grid Systems The Amish Have Used" 332K (4mo) and 256K (3mo). Off-grid water lens only.
4. **Possible: SoftPro Water Systems** (a dealer; its presenter looks AI-generated, unconfirmed). "The Government Is NOT Testing Your Well Water (You Are)" 467K (6mo), but only 133 subs, buried under spam city pages.
5. **Adjacent, not water-specific:** Keiko Sato (an invented "74-year-old"; copper + zinc water 33K) and The Retired Plumber / Roy Kessler (AI plumber personas, under 20 subs, general plumbing).

**Nobody has an AI or invented veteran persona covering water quality in the home** (filters, softeners, tap vs bottled, testing, PFAS, salesman tactics).

### (4) Is any channel a veteran plumber or water-tech persona giving verdicts?

- **Real people, not personas:**
  - The Grumpy Plumber (48-year master plumber, face, 86.8K): softener verdicts 167K and 102K, but 3-5 years old and not a water channel.
  - Mid Atlantic Water (dealer owner, face, "32 years"): "What I'd Actually Buy After 32 Years", but only 1.5-9K views.
  - Water Filter Guru (WQA-certified specialist, face): "Unbiased, Data-Driven" verdicts at 100-260K.
  - H²O Mechanic (well tech, face): repair vlogs, not verdicts.
- **Personas:** only **The Off-Grid Well Finder** (AI "42-year driller") uses the veteran-verdict format ("9 Things We Never Told Our Customers", "Only 3 I'd Put in My Own Well"). Its one hit is 161K and the rest are small.
- **Conclusion:** no veteran **plumber** persona gives filter, softener or tap-water verdicts. The "only N I'd buy" format, which runs at 14-42x for appliances in lane 6, is **unused for water filters and softeners**.
