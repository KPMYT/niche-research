# Lane 7: weird, creative and format-led Roger sub-brands

Researched 2026-10-22 with NexLev (search_niche_finder_channels, faceless_outliers_videos, youtube_search, youtube_channel_outliers, search_viral_videos_small_channels).

**How to read the numbers**
- "Nx" is NexLev's outlier score: the video's views divided by the channel's average.
- RPM is NexLev's estimated total RPM for that channel.
- Ages are YouTube's relative text ("6mo ago"). Niche-finder upload dates are rounded, so treat those ages as approximate.
- Every number below came from a tool result. Nothing is estimated by me unless it is labelled as my estimate.

**Ranking logic**
1. Fit with Roger's audience: US men 55-75 who own an older home, are nostalgic, distrust upsells and are self-reliant.
2. How open the gap is: is there no strong channel with a persona yet?
3. How naturally Roger can hand his audience over.
