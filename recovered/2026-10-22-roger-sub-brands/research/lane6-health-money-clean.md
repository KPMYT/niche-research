# Lane 6: Non-trade topics with a "plumber's mind" bend (health, water, cleaning, money/scams, kitchen, car)

Research date: 2026-10-22. Tools: NexLev `search_niche_finder_channels` (11 queries), `youtube_search` (10 queries), `search_viral_videos_small_channels` (5 queries), `youtube_channel_outliers` (4 channels), `search_videos` (4 queries, which returned mostly irrelevant mega-views noise and were discarded).

**How to read the numbers**
- Subs, views, revenue and RPM are copied from NexLev results.
- Video ages: `youtube_search` and `youtube_channel_outliers` give ages directly ("2mo ago"). For niche-finder rows, NexLev gives rounded upload dates, so ages are shown with "~" and counted back from 2026-10-22.
- "Outlier" is the NexLev view-ratio against the channel average where the tool gave one. "Ch. outlier score" is NexLev's channel-level score (0 = average, 2+ = strong).
- NexLev RPM is an estimate. It swings a lot between channels in the same niche, so treat ranges as directional.
