# Panel 3: the Autistic Systemizer (network flow)

**My question:** for each candidate, how much of Roger's audience flows in, how much new audience it pulls back into the Roger network, and what it takes from Roger and the operator's other channels.

**Net flow = inflow from Roger + new audience imported + return flow to Roger and the book − cannibalisation (of Roger, of the operator's plumbing siblings, of the operator's other channels).** Each candidate gets one score from 1 to 10.

## 0. The network laws the evidence supports (these drive every score)

**L1. A name is a weak edge.**
- Eli Yoder Secrets gains ~29.3K subs a month. Its spin-off Eli Yoder The Plumber gains ~590 a month, and Martha Yoder Secrets has 104 subs.
- Home Hacks with Uncle Kenji has 4.1K subs but only 6,887 views in total.
- The Otis Granger siblings all launched on the same day under the same name and ended up at 45.5K, 31.6K, 9.12K and 5.32K. With the name held equal, topic decided the outcome.

**L2. Topic is the strong edge, and the recommender sorts by topic, not by brand.**
- In Roger's similar graph, **Eli Yoder The Plumber has a similarity of 67 to Roger, while its own parent, Eli Yoder Secrets, has only 31.**
- So the spin-off was not placed next to its parent. It was pulled into the plumbing cluster, where it sits among roughly 25 plumbing nodes (Roger, Mark, Vintage, Holic Clever, Roger Wakefield, mmplumber, etc.) and gets about 4.6K views per video.
- **A sub-brand that sits on Roger's topic lands in Roger's cluster and competes with Roger.**

**L3. Spin-offs that work leave the parent's cluster for a neighbouring one the parent's viewers already visit.**
- Bertha's Clean Home (~31K/mo) led to Bertha's House Hacks (~59K/mo).
- Otis Granger's main channel (tools, 31.6K) led to Otis's Kitchen (45.5K in 9 weeks).
- In both cases the sibling outgrew the original, and in both it moved toward the household and kitchen pool, which leans female.

**L4. The operator's plumbing cluster is already crowded with the operator's own channels.**
- Mark Silverbridge sits at similarity 83 to Roger and The Vintage Pipefitter at 76.
- The registry records winning titles re-made 1:1 on all three faceless plumbing channels.
- Werner works the same pool through the German lens.
- Any new sub-brand about pipes would be the 5th node competing for the same impressions.

**L5. Introducing a character first is the only hand-off the operator controls. It has never been measured.**
- The registry says Werner's "Roger collab tag" is **still open**.
- Collab bylines do exist in this ecosystem (Frannie's byline "and Vintage Lifestyle USA"; The Senior Traveler × Senior Flights cross-crediting).
- No conversion rate exists anywhere in the research. I will not invent one.

**L6. Seasons shift the flow.**
- Winter peaks: heat, freeze-ups and blizzards (B, AB, E).
- Spring and summer peaks: pests, lawn, RV de-winterizing and travel, BBQ (F, N, AC, T).
- Pairing nodes that peak in opposite seasons keeps the network's flow steady across the year.
