Research date: 2026-10-22. Sources: NexLev `search_niche_finder_channels`, `faceless_outliers_videos`, `youtube_channel_outliers`, and live `youtube_search` (US).

**How to read the numbers**
- **RPM:** "RPM base" is NexLev's niche RPM estimate per channel, in USD.
- **Rev:** NexLev's estimated AdSense per month.
- **Outlier "x":** from `youtube_channel_outliers` (views divided by the channel average) or from `faceless_outliers_videos` (outlierScore).
- **Ages:** these come from YouTube "published" text, or are month-level dates from NexLev's channel listings. Treat the NexLev dates as approximate.
- Every number below was seen in a tool result. Where something was not measured, it says so.

## 1. Pest control: "Roger's Brother Buck, the Bug Man" (or "Roger the Bug Guy")

**Names**
- "Buck the Bug Man", Roger's younger brother and a 40-year exterminator. Same blunt voice and the same distrust of the monthly-contract upsell.
- Alternatives: "Roger's Bug Book" (mirrors his book brand) or "Roger the Bug Guy".

**Concept:** a retired exterminator gives away what the $59-a-month spray truck does. The core is cheap substances that kill or repel mice, roaches, ants, wasps, mosquitoes, termites and bed bugs, plus "what exterminators do in their own homes."

**Why this audience watches:** Roger's biggest levers are cheap-substance mysteries, "what pros do in their own homes" and distrust of trades. In this niche those levers are the whole niche. ## 2. Old house, basement and water damage: "Roger's Old House" (or "Roger's Basement")

**Names**
- "Roger's Old House": extends his book "100 Old-House Fixes".
- "Roger the Basement Guy", or "Roger's Crawlspace".
