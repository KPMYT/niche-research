## First minute (reading)

The two explainers that did well open on a named disaster plus a "how do they know?" question: Primal Space gives a date, the disaster and a "7-month rabbit hole" teaser inside 45 seconds, then the method (V burn patterns, char depth). Thumbnails: a single question ("WHERE DID IT START?", "who started this?") over a scene. The true-crime ones open on a death toll and live on their brand.

## Gap (reading)

1. People want the investigator's method when it's tied to a famous fire. No channel connects that method to the viewer's own house.
2. Persona attempts so far put "investigator" in the title over ordinary NEVER-lists; they perform like the firefighter lists (about 100-300 views). The label alone adds nothing measurable.
3. Electrical-cause demand exists but sits with electricians and news: FixItWithFil 274,756; John Ward 275,703; Will Prowse 503,205. Nobody owns an electrical "fire started here" walk-through format.
4. Weak point: 9 of the top 10 rely on real crime and big brands, which a persona can't borrow.
