## Problems

1. The Popular page is a channel's 30 most-viewed, not its whole catalogue (Silver Cymbal shows 4 in scope of 21 there, against 150 in round 1). Big general channels in the top 30 got there on one or two wire-trick or how-to hits.
2. Some false hits probably remain (hack channels, electric-bill money tricks).
3. Logged-out English pages translate some foreign titles; 11 marked out by description language. Electric Online (Pakistan) and TOP HEIGHTS SOLAR (Nigeria) stay in as non-US.
4. 58 core channels have no first-upload date in any source ("not found").
5. About a dozen page loads failed and were retried (files overwritten).
