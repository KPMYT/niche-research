## New emotions proposed (no E-code fit)

- **N1 nuisance irritation:** a household annoyance the viewer wants stopped now (the chirp, the false alarm, the beep). Not danger; not failure unless the title says the fix didn't work (then E6 first).
- **N2 how-it-works curiosity:** wanting to understand a system ("explained", "how X works", "basics", "types of") with no secret or "real reason" angle. E10 kept for "actually", "the real reason", "not what you think" and everyday "why" questions.
- **N3 spectacle and thrill:** watching something burn, spark or explode for entertainment (flashover demos, MythBusters, near-misses on camera, torture tests).
