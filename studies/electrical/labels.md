# Labels: electrical and fire prevention study

The same labels as the HVAC study (`RESEARCH-BRIEF.md` section 3). New labels are added at the bottom, marked **NEW**, with the date and the reason. A label is always a reading of the title, the thumbnail and (where checked) the video, not a measurement, unless a row says it was counted.

## Presenter background (for the open decision on Frederick)

electrician · lineman / utility · firefighter · fire inspector / marshal · home inspector · other trade (say which) · none stated. "Claimed" = what the channel says; we can't verify it.

## The multiple

Views divided by the median views of that channel's long-form videos (5 minutes or more) from the last 12 months; if it has fewer than 10, its last 30 long-form videos. Each table says which was used.

## Length rule

Counted videos are long-form: 5:00 or more, in English, no Shorts. Videos under 5:00 are kept only in the separate "short reference" list (`short-reference.md`), labelled but never counted (the operator, 2026-10-23: "yes").
