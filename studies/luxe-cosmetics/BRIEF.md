## What each product study answers

1. What the product is, in detail: format, size, price (and price history if found), ingredients (full INCI list where found), claims, how to use, how long it lasts, where it's sold.
2. Whether it's used with other products or as one step of a routine, and which step.
3. Who it's for (age, skin or lash type, problem, budget). Note in particular how it fits women over 50 and 60: the operator's style channels (Scarlett Starling, Diane de Chambray) speak to that audience.
4. Every problem it solves and how, and how it does it better or differently from other brands.
5. How it's positioned against competitors: a table of the main rivals (brand, product, price, size, price per mL or pad, key ingredient, rating and review count, positioning).
6. User feedback: the biggest problems buyers had before; how the product solved them; complaints and failures; other feedback of note. Quote real reviews with source and date. Count themes where you can.
7. The category as a whole: what the category is, how it works (the science, plainly), what the evidence says, the common pains and fears of buyers, myths, the safety issues, and what viewers search for and ask.
8. Anything else the operator needs to sell it honestly on YouTube: claims they can and can't make, safety warnings, who should not use it, red flags in reviews.
