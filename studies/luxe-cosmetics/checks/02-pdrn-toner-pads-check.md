## 2. Ingredient list vs Anua: confirmed (with a nuance)

- **Luxe list.** I loaded the live image https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0718/0379/7810/files/Luxe_GelPad_Imagev8.png?v=1785837013 and read it myself. It matches the report's transcription word for word: 45 ingredients, Aqua (Water) to Oligopeptide-3.
- **Anua, dodoskin** (https://www.dodoskin.com/products/anua-pdrn-100-hyaluronic-acid-glow-pad-60p.json, $21.78): 45 ingredients. A script compared the two lists line by line. 32 of 45 names are identical. The other 13 differ only by name form: Purified water / Aqua; "pcai" typo; "indigo plant" for Melia Azadirachta (x2); ivy gourd / Coccinia Indica; aloe vera / Aloe Barbadensis; eggplant; coptis; holy basil / Ocimum Sanctum; corn; "coral lily" / Corallina Officinalis; Curcuma longa; kakadu plum / Terminalia Ferdinandiana. **The order is identical at all 45 positions.** "sodium DNA (100 ppm)", "sodium hyaluronate (5.002 ppm)" and "hyaluronic acid (5 ppm)" are confirmed.
- **Anua's own ingredient image** (https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0753/1429/9158/files/anua-us-toner-60-ea-pdrn-100-hyaluronic-acid-glow-pad-1170826103.png?v=1757400043): the same Latin names, **in the same order as Luxe's**, in the same "Ingredient List" layout. This is the strongest match: Luxe's image reproduces Anua's.
- **Nuance the report missed.** The text ingredient list on Anua's own product page (https://anua.us/products/pdrn-100-hyaluronic-acid-glow-pad, which redirects to anua.com) and on INCIDecoder (https://incidecoder.com/products/anua-pdrn-100-hyaluronic-acid-glow-pad) has **the same 45 ingredients in a different order**: only 13 of 45 positions match, and Sodium DNA comes *before* Disodium EDTA there. "Same 45 ingredients" holds against every Anua source. "Same order" holds against Anua's ingredient image and dodoskin, not against anua.com's text list. This also weakens the report's inference that the PDRN is below the EDTA level (section 1.4): on Anua's own text list, sodium DNA sits above disodium EDTA. The 100 ppm figure still comes from dodoskin.
