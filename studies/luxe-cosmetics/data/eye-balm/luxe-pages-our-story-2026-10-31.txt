Our Story
Welcome to Luxe Cosmetics, your one-stop shop for revolutionary beauty products. We're changing what's perceived as possible, from the comfort of your own home.
OUR BIGGEST WHY
The modern beauty industry can often be harmful to our physical and mental health, as well as the environment. Luxe Cosmetics believes that no one should give up their health or principles to look beautiful. In fact, we believe that the healthier and more efficient you are, the better you feel. That's why we wanted to take a different approach to beauty.
We strongly believe in natural, simple and instant beauty. After all, everyone would love to wake up looking AWESOME every morning, and we strive to make that a reality for everyone.
Our mission is to create easy-care, no-fuss products that let you look confident day after day.
That's why we produce high-quality cosmetics for eyelashes and eyebrows. Our products have won several awards and are laboratory tested. All our products are vegan and cruelty-free.
Every day we strive to improve every aspect of our brand and our customer experience. If you feel we can improve in any area, whatever it is, please contact us! We're always open to new ideas and criticism and would love to hear what you have to say!
