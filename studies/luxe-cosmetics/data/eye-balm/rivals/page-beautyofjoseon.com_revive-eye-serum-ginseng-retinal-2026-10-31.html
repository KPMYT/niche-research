FAQ

What skin types can use this product?
This eye serum is designed to work for all skin types. Sensitive skin types should begin with a small amount and increase the amount of product gradually.
Can I put this product over my entire face?
Our Revive Eye Serum is safe to use on your entire face but is primarily designed for use on the under-eye area.
Can pregnant women use this product?
Revive Eye Serum contains retinal, an ingredient that pregnant women should avoid. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, please avoid using this product.
Can I use it along with AHA/BHA/PHA products?
Revive Eye Serum contains a powerful ingredient, retinal lipsomes, which helps address signs of aging like fine lines and skin texture. To reduce the risk of irritation, we don't recommend mixing it with AHA/BHA/PHA.
Can I use it both day and night?
Yes! You can use our Revive Eye Serum in both your AM and PM skincare routines. If applying during the day, make sure to follow with sunscreen.
I'm feeling a stinging sensation. Is this normal?
Some skin types can be sensitive to retinal. If you have especially dry or sensitive skin, we recommend layering this eye cream over a gentle moisturizing cream.
