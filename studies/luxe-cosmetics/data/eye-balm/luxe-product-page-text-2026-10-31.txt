Frequently Asked Questions
How quickly will I see results?
Most women see visible smoothing in 7-10 days. Puffiness and dark circles typically improve first, followed by smoother texture and reduced fine lines over 3-4 weeks. The full collagen response peaks around day 28.
Is it safe for sensitive skin?
Yes. There's no retinol. No fragrance. No parabens. No artificial colors. No essential oils. Bisabolol and Vitamin E are in the formula specifically to calm reactive skin. If you have specific concerns, we recommend patch testing first.
Can I wear it under makeup?
Yes. The lightweight formula absorbs in about 8 seconds and sets matte. Most customers report their concealer stops creasing within the first week.
How long does one tube last?
About 60-75 days with twice-daily use. The BOGO gives you 4-5 months of supply.
What's your return policy?
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with your results for any reason, contact our team within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. No forms, no fine print, no hassle.
What are the key ingredients?
Adenosine — clinically proven to reduce fine lines and improve skin elasticity. Niacinamide — brightens dark circles and evens skin tone. Bisabolol — soothes and calms sensitive skin. Vitamin E — protects and nourishes. Hyaluronic Acid — deep, lasting hydration.
Can I use this with my retinol or vitamin C?
Yes. Use Luxe in the morning, your other actives at night. Most women find they don't need the others once adenosine takes over the collagen work.
How is this different from TULA, Kahi, or La Mer?
TULA gives an instant pink-tint glow but has no long-term collagen active. Kahi has PDRN but no adenosine. La Mer is mostly heritage pricing. Luxe is the first balm that stacks adenosine with four supporting actives at masstige pricing. You're paying for the formulation, not the brand name.
