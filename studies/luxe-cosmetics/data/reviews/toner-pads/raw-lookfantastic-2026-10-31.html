Serum-infused pads that provide plumping moisture and a glass skin glow.

Dual-sided, the Anua PDRN 100 Hyaluronic Acid Glow Pads feature a gel peptide mask side for luminous nourishment and a smoothing fabric side to wipe away excess and lock in moisture after use.

Infused with a salmon DNA-derived compound (PDRN) known for its ability to reduce dullness, the serum-saturated pads encourage an instant glass skin glow. A blend of hyaluronic acid and peptides helps to plump the skin with moisture, minimising visible fine lines to support a smooth, supple look. Applied as a five-minute treatment, the pads can be adhered to areas of concern for targeted use.

  • ☼ AM / ☽ PM​
  • When to Use: After cleansing.
  • Application: Apply the peptide gel side to your skin and leave it on for five minutes like a mini sheet mask. Then, use the plain side to gently wipe along your skin texture and lightly pat in the remaining essence.
  • PDRN: A salmon DNA-derived compound that enhances luminosity
  • Hyaluronic Acid: A humectant that supports long-lasting hydration
  • Peptides: Short chains of amino acids that promote firmer-looking skin
