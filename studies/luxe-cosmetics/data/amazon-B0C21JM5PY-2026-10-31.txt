 About this item 
 ✨ THICKER, LONGER AND FULLER LASHES: Luxe Cosmetics' Eyelash Serum is specially formulated to help you achieve thicker, longer, and fuller eyelashes. Whether you have naturally thin eyelashes or you've overplucked in the past, this serum can help you achieve the bold and beautiful lashes you desire.  
 ✨VISIBLE RESULTS IN JUST 4 WEEKS: With regular use, you can expect to see visible results in as little as 4 weeks. Say goodbye to thin lashes and hello to a more defined and voluminous look.  
 ✨ VEGAN FORMULA: Vegan and Cruelty-Free Formula, at Luxe Cosmetics, we believe in using only the highest quality, ethically sourced ingredients. That's why our Eyelash Serum is 100% vegan and cruelty-free, so you can feel good about using it on your skin.  
 ✨RESTORE NATURAL GROWTH: This Eyelash serum is formulated with ingredients that help to stimulate natural hair growth, so you can achieve fuller lashes without the need for expensive and time-consuming treatments. Additionally, it can help reduce hair loss and promote healthier hair growth.  
 ✨ ABOUT LUXE COSMETICS: Our main goal is to enhance your beauty using the best ingredients to ensure that all our products are designed under the highest quality process to get the better results. Discover all our products made with top quality and gentle ingredients to elevate your confidence and beauty.
