Does it have any side effects?
Our formula is specially formulated to be gentle and safe for everyone, including cancer patients! Our products contain no parabens and are also gluten-free and vegan friendly. However, you should avoid getting the formula in your eyes. In case of eye contact, you should rinse your eyes thoroughly with water.
Also, you should only use Luxe Eyelash Serum under the supervision of your doctor if you are undergoing treatment for an eye condition. Keep out of reach of children. We recommend that you wash your hands and face with mild soap and water before use.
Can I use it during pregnancy or breastfeeding?
If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, we recommend you consult your doctor before use.
How long does a tube of Luxe Eyelash Serum last?
One tube of our Luxe Eyelash Serum lasts for 1-2 months, depending on how often you use the serum. Pro tip: We recommend using the serum twice a day for the first 2 months for best results! Also, be sure to replace old bottles every 2 months for maximum results.

How should I store the serums?
Please make sure to replace old bottles every 2 months for maximum results. Our serums do not contain preservatives, so you have a shelf life of 4 months. We recommend using your Luxe Serums within 1-2 months, storing them at 36°F to 77°F (2.2°C to 25°C) and tightly closing the lid after use.
