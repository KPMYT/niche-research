 How To Use
The 10-second nightly routine:
Step 1:
 Remove all eye makeup with an oil-free cleanser or micellar water.
Step 2:
 Dip the applicator wand once — this is enough for both eyes.
Step 3:
 Apply a thin line along your upper lash line, as close to the roots as possible (like you're applying liquid eyeliner).
Step 4:
 Let dry for 1-2 minutes before applying any other products. Go to sleep — the serum works overnight.
That's it.
 Total time: under 10 seconds.
Pro tips for best results:
- Apply once daily, preferably at night before bed
- Be consistent — the formula needs 4-6 weeks to show visible results
- Don't apply to lower lashes (the serum will naturally transfer when you blink)
- If you miss a day, just apply the next night — don't double up
How long does one bottle last?
One 3mL bottle contains approximately 60 applications (2 months of daily use).
