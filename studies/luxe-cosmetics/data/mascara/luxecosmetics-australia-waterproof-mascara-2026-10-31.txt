Will this actually work on short, thin lashes?
Yes
— and here's why it works when others haven't.
Most mascaras pile on heavy pigment to create the illusion of volume. On thick, young lashes, that works. On thinner, shorter lashes after 50, it just clumps, flakes, and weighs them down.
This formula does the opposite.
It's featherlight. The wand separates and grips every lash individually. Two coats give you visible length and soft, natural fullness without any of the heaviness.
And because the formula is water-based and pH balanced, it doesn't sting, dry out your lashes, or irritate sensitive eyes. It actually heals you while you wear it.
It works on every skin tone, every complexion, every lash type — whether you're 50, 60, 70, or beyond.
How is this different from others?
This mascara was built from the ground up for lashes that changed after 50.
1. The wand actually grips short, fine lashes.
This one catches and separates every single lash — including the tiny ones in the corners that usually get missed.
2. The formula is featherlight — not heavy.
Regular mascaras pile on thick pigment to fake volume. Luxe's formula wraps each lash in a thin, buildable coat. You get visible fullness and length without any of the heaviness.
3. It won't sting, smudge, flake, or settle into your fine lines.
The formula is hypoallergenic and pH balanced — designed for the most sensitive eyes. It stays put all day. No midday touch-ups.

Most mascaras dry your lashes out and cause breakage over time. Luxe's nourishes, hydrate and strengthen your natural lashes while you wear it. The more you use it, the healthier your lashes get.
5. It comes off with warm water — no damage.
No waterproof formulas that require harsh removers and aggressive scrubbing. Warm water dissolves it cleanly. No tugging. No pulling out lashes. No undoing all the growth you've built.
