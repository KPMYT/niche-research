PRODUCT DETAILS
Pump up your lash routine with L’Oréal Voluminous Lash Paradise Washable Mascara, the ultimate tool for instant volume and length. No clumping, no smudging, no flaking—just long and luscious lashes that last up to 24 hours. Infused with nourishing floral oil, this washable mascara leaves lashes conditioned and soft to the touch. The ultra-soft wavy brush catches every lash for dense, fuller-looking lashes, adding 20 times more volume and +98% lengthening effect. Ophthalmologist-tested and safe for sensitive eyes and contact lens wearers.
Product Highlights:
Volumizing and Lengthening Formula:
Achieve a full lash fringe that's feathery soft with no clumping. Lash Paradise mascara provides up to 20x more volume and 2X more length, according to a consumer study.
Iconic Ultra-Soft Wavy Brush:
The specially designed mascara brush with 200+ bristles captures every lash effortlessly, delivering dramatic volume and length with precision.
Soft Conditioning Formula:
Lashes are nourished with our floral oil-infused formula, making them feel conditioned and soft for up to 24 hours.
Long-Lasting and Smudge-Resistant:
Bold, dense lashes stay flawless for up to 24 hours without smudging or flaking.
Suitable for Sensitive Eyes:
This gentle, fragrance-free, washable formula is perfect for everyone, including contact lens wearers and those with sensitive eyes. Also available in

Why Choose Lash Paradise?
Longwearing yet washable, Voluminous Lash Paradise Mascara is designed for those seeking intense length and maximal volume. Its clump-free, smudge-free, flake-free formula is infused with floral oil for an added boost of softness that lasts all day.
What It Helps With:
Instant Volume and Length:
Experience dramatic lashes thanks to a high-impact volumizing and lengthening formula and 200+ bristle lash brush.
Supple and Soft Lashes:
Infused with floral oil so lashes feel conditioned and soft to the touch.
Smudge-Resistant, Long-Lasting Wear:
Keep your lashes looking pristine all day with a mascara that resists smudging and clumping.

WHO SHOULD USE IT
Perfect for those seeking instant, dramatic volume and length without a stiff or heavy feel, Lash Paradise Mascara gives individuals with sparse or fine lashes a "full-fringe" look. This formula is a favorite for those with sensitive eyes, and those who require an accessible luxury mascara that delivers high-impact results with a gentle touch.
Also available in a
waterproof version

HOW TO USE (ROUTINE STEPS)
Incorporate this washable mascara into your daily routine as your primary lash lengthener and volumizer, following these easy steps:
Step 1: Prime.
For maximum thickness and enhanced mascara wear, start your process with
Lash Paradise Mascara Primer/Base
, coating lashes from base to tip for maximum mascara adherence.
Step 2: Coat.
Position the Voluminous Lash Paradise Mascara wand at the base of your lashes and gently sweep upward from root to tip, wiggling slightly for even and thorough coverage.
Step 3: Build.
For dramatically full and voluminous lashes, layer multiple coats while the mascara is still wet.
Step 4: Remove.
To take off the mascara, wash gently with soap and water or use a makeup remover like micellar water.
Pro Tip:
For extra-gentle removal, soak a cotton pad in makeup remover, press onto closed eyes for 10–20 seconds to dissolve formula, and wipe gently downward.

Why it matters:
Helps to condition and care for your lashes, contributing to a soft feel and healthy appearance. Its emollient properties can also aid in a smooth, clump-free application, ensuring your lashes look lush and defined from root to tip.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Is Lash Paradise mascara safe for sensitive eyes?
Yes, L'Oréal Paris Lash Paradise is ophthalmologist-tested and suitable for sensitive eyes and contact lens wearers. The formula is gentle yet high-performing.
How do I remove Lash Paradise Washable Mascara without damaging my lashes?
Because this is a washable mascara formula, it can be easily removed with soap and water or your favorite Micellar Water. No harsh scrubbing is required, preserving your natural lash fringe.
What is the difference between Lash Paradise and Voluminous Original?
While both provide incredible volume, Lash Paradise features a soft wavy brush with over 200 bristles to catch every lash for a "feather-soft" fringe effect, and the formula is infused with floral oils for conditioning.
Does Lash Paradise mascara flake or smudge throughout the day?
Our smudge-resistant formula is designed to stay in place, providing long-wear intensity and volume without the flaking often associated with high-impact mascaras.
What’s the difference between the washable and waterproof versions?
The washable version of Voluminous Lash Paradise mascara is best for sensitive eyes and contact lens wearers, as it’s easy to remove with soap and water or makeup remover. The
waterproof version
provides additional staying power for humid or wet conditions (read: it’s cry-proof!). Both formulas feature the same conditioning floral oil infusion and ultra-soft wavy brush, catching every lash for intense volume and length.
