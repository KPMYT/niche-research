Frequently Asked Questions
What is tubing mascara?
Liquid Lash™ Extensions Mascara’s award-winning tubing formula precisely wraps each lash in lightweight ‘tubes’ that build and extend beyond the lash for maximum length! These tubes are water-resistant, smudge-proof, and last all day without flaking while also being gentle on lashes and sensitive eyes. They will loosen up + remove from lashes effortlessly when soaked with warm water. If you’re still new to the wonderful world of tubing mascara, check out our detailed blog post,
What is Tubing Mascara
for more in-depth info!
Is our tubing mascara waterproof?
Our Liquid Lash™ Extensions Mascara is water-resistant and will last all day without clumping, streaking, or flaking. Our innovative tubing mascara formula holds up beautifully through rain, sweat, tears, and other daily activities that may cause your eyes to water. We designed it this way so that it's easy to apply and remove.
How should I apply the tubing formula for optimal results?
Our unique tubing formula will wrap around each individual lash and sets as soon as it dries down. For optimal performance, we recommend focusing on one eye at a time. You can apply layer after layer while the mascara is still wet, until you achieve your preferred length + volume. Explore our guide on
how to make your eyelashes look longer
.
How can I keep my tubing mascara performing its best?
To maintain smooth application and long-lasting wear, keep the cap tightly closed between uses, store your tubing mascara at room temperature, and avoid pumping the wand in and out of the tube. These simple habits can help preserve the formula and prevent it from drying out prematurely. For more tips and insights on keeping mascara fresh, read our guide on
how long mascara lasts
.
Is tubing mascara better than regular mascara?
Unlike traditional formulas that coat lashes with pigment, tubing mascaras create tiny, water-resistant "tubes" around each lash, making them smudge-proof and long-wearing. They’re great if you struggle with raccoon eyes or have oily skin. Plus, they’re easy to remove with warm water and gentle pressure—no harsh makeup remover needed. If you prioritize longevity, easy removal, or have sensitive eyes, tubing mascara might be worth the switch!
How can you tell if a mascara is tubing?
To tell if mascara is a tubing formula, check the product description for phrases like "tubing formula," "water-resistant tubes," or "smudge-proof." Many tubing mascaras highlight easy removal with just warm water. Look for polymers in the ingredient list, as they create the tube-like effect around each lash. You can also test it: apply the mascara, then remove it with warm water—if it slides off in little tubes or clumps instead of smearing, it’s a tubing mascara!
Is tubing mascara hard to remove?
Our favorite thing about our tubing formula is how easy it is to remove. All you need is warm water, and the tubes will slide right off. Saturate a cotton pad with warm water and let it sit on your lashes for 30 seconds. Next, using your fingers or the cotton pad, slowly move in downward swiping motions. Alternatively, you can just wet your fingers with warm water and very gently slide the tubes down and off of your lashes. Check out our post on
How to Remove Tubing Mascara
for more in-depth steps.
Why wear grey mascara instead of black?
Charcoal grey mascara defines lashes in a softer, more understated way than black. It adds length and lift without the harsh contrast, making it ideal for everyday looks or for anyone who wants a more wearable, natural-looking lash. Especially flattering on thinning lashes or lighter colored hair, grey effortlessly enhances your eyes without overpowering your features. Learn more about our
charcoal grey tubing mascara shade
.
Is grey mascara only for people with grey or silver hair?
Not at all! While grey mascara is especially flattering on silver and grey hair, it’s a versatile shade that suits everyone. Its cool undertones bring out the whites in your eyes and work beautifully on every complexion. It’s a great option for anyone looking to soften their lash look or experiment beyond rich black!
