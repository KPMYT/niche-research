DO NOT BUY!
I rarely leave reviews, but I feel like I have to warn others about this lash lifting kit.
The first time I used it, I followed the instructions carefully and even did a patch test on the back of my hand the day before, which was absolutely fine. However, after using it on my lashes, my eyes became extremely red, swollen, and unbearably itchy. It got so bad that I had to go to A&E for treatment.
Once the swelling finally went down, I was left with eyelashes that were completely ruined. They looked burnt — dry, brittle, and sticking out in all directions. It was honestly embarrassing.
I waited around 8 weeks before trying again (mainly because I felt insecure about how bad my lashes looked after the first time). At first, they seemed okay, but then the exact same thing happened — they became dry, damaged, and lifeless even after oiling every morning and night. Now, about 4 weeks later, my lashes have started falling out.
Before using this kit, I had naturally long, healthy lashes. Now they’re sparse, brittle, and actually uncomfortable — my eyes feel irritated every time I blink because of how dry and jagged my lashes have become.
I originally bought this on a buy-one-get-one-free deal, but I’ve thrown away both the used kit and the unopened one because I wouldn’t risk this happening again.
My lashes are completely ruined. Please do not buy this product.
