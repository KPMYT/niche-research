# Luxe PDRN Toner Pads: the short version

*Product 2. Prices as of 2026-10-31.*

## 1. In one sentence

A jar of 140 soaked gel pads you lay on your face for 5 minutes like a mini mask, then wipe with: a gentle hydration (water) step that makes skin look plumper and dewier for hours.

## 2. What it is, in detail

- **Format:** a pink 200 g tub of 140 round pads (7 cm, about a coffee mug's base) in a wobbly pink gel, with tweezers. A slippery **gel side** for masking, a **fabric side** for wiping. Made in Korea.
- **Price:** $59.95 on the brand site = **$0.43 a pad**. Lasts 70 days at two a day.
- **Smell:** no fragrance listed; almost none, say buyers of the identical Anua pad.

| Main ingredient | What it is | What it does here |
|---|---|---|
| Water, glycerin, butylene glycol, glycereth-26 | The base, about 80–90% of the pad | **Humectants** (water-grabbers, like a sponge). Pull water into the top layer of skin |
| **Niacinamide** | Vitamin B3, 4th on the list | The one well-proven ingredient. In studies (at 5%) it softened the look of fine lines and dark spots over 4–12 weeks. Amount here not stated |
| Adenosine | A natural molecule | A Korean-approved anti-wrinkle ingredient. Small amount |
| **PDRN ("sodium DNA")** | Small pieces of salmon DNA | The headline ingredient. About 0.01% (100 ppm, parts per million). A light surface moisturiser |
| Hyaluronic acid, peptides, collagen | Water-holders and protein pieces | Tiny amounts. Collagen on the skin does not become your collagen |
| Plant extracts (neem, turmeric, kakadu plum, soy ferment) | Small amounts | Antioxidant (protects from sun and pollution) and soothing claims, mostly lab-based |

**Not in it:** exfoliating acids, retinol (vitamin A), vitamin C, fragrance, drying alcohol, oils or ceramides (barrier fats).

## 3. How this kind of product works

- **Skin basics.** The top layer is a "brick wall": dead cells are the bricks, fats the mortar. This is the **skin barrier**; it keeps water in and irritants out. Pads only touch this layer. Wrinkles and sagging start deeper.
- **Hydration vs moisture.** Hydration = water. Moisture = the oils that seal it in. This pad adds water but no seal, so it **needs a moisturiser on top**.
- **Toner** = any watery liquid used after washing. Three kinds: **astringent** (alcohol, drying), **exfoliating** (acids that loosen dead cells) and **hydrating**. **Luxe is hydrating**, the kind dermatologists (skin doctors) recommend after 50.
- **Toner pads** = toner pre-soaked into pads in a jar. Types: exfoliating, hydrating, **gel** (sits flat like a mini sheet mask), dual-sided, PDRN, retinol and DIY. Luxe is a **dual-sided hydrating gel PDRN pad**.
- **PDRN** (polydeoxyribonucleotide) is DNA, mostly from salmon sperm. **Injected**, it helps wounds heal. On the skin, the pieces are far too big to get in, like a football through a letterbox. Dr Dray (dermatologist) doubts PDRN products "do anything above and beyond moisturize the skin".
- **Why it's trending:** Kim Kardashian's "salmon sperm facial" injections, Korean skincare ("K-beauty") and "glass skin" (very smooth, dewy skin). Online interest in PDRN rose about 610% in a year.

## 4. How it's used, and with what

**The steps:**
1. Wash your face. Pat almost dry.
2. Lift out one pad with the tweezers. Close the lid at once.
3. Lay the **gel side** on a cheek or forehead for **5 minutes**.
4. Turn it over and **gently** sweep the fabric side over the face, avoiding eyes and lips. Never scrub.
5. Pat in the rest. Wait a minute, then **moisturiser**; in the morning, **sunscreen** last.

Or: wipe only (30 seconds), or mask only (calming).

| Step | Morning | Night |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rinse or gentle cleanser | Remove makeup, then gentle cleanser |
| 2 | **Luxe pad** | **Luxe pad** |
| 3 | Optional serum | Optional retinol some nights |
| 4 | Moisturiser | Richer moisturiser |
| 5 | Sunscreen SPF 30+, then makeup | — |

- **Pairs well with:** a ceramide moisturiser, sunscreen, retinol (the pad adds no sting), vitamin C serum, makeup.
- **Be careful with:** acid pads or scrubs the same day; prescription creams for eczema (itchy, dry patches) or rosacea (long-lasting redness): ask the doctor.
- **Don't:** reuse a pad, leave it on 20–30 minutes, or put makeup on while wet (it rolls into crumbs, called **pilling**).

## 5. Who it's for

**Good fit:** dry, dehydrated (tight, dull) or normal skin; people who can't use retinol, acids or fragrance; anyone wanting a glow before makeup.

**How needs change after 50 and 60.** Menopause lowers estrogen, and skin changes:

| Change after 50 | Can the pad help? |
|---|---|
| Dryness: "probably the most striking change" (Dr Dray) | **Yes, its main strength**, with a moisturiser |
| Weaker barrier; old products start to sting | Partly: no acids, fragrance or alcohol |
| Dullness (dead cells pile up) | Yes, short term |
| Fine lines | Plumps them for hours |
| Collagen loss (about 30% in 5 years), crepey skin | No real change |
| Age spots | Slowly, maybe (niacinamide), only with daily sunscreen |
| Sagging, jowls (bone and fat change) | No |

After 60, skin is thinner still: **press rather than wipe**, use a richer cream; the 5-minute mask suits best.

**Not for:** **fish allergy** (salmon DNA) or soy allergy; vegans; very oily or acne-prone skin; anyone wanting lifting or firming; tight budgets.

## 8. What users say

No independent Luxe reviews exist yet, so this is the identical Anua pad and close rivals.

**Biggest problems before:** dry skin after menopause; dullness; fine lines; skin that reacts to retinol and acids; makeup clinging to dry patches; PDRN products that "didn't live up to the hype".

**How it solved them:** instant water. Of 73 Anua reviews, 38 say "hydrating", 25 mention glow, 19 use it as a mask, 12 say makeup goes on better. A few report softer fine lines over time. None mention dark spots fading.

## 10. What you can and can't say on camera

- **Can say:** "a gentle, fragrance-free, acid-free hydrating gel pad"; "skin looks plumper and dewier right after"; "use it as a 5-minute mini mask".
- **Can say:** "contains niacinamide, which can help skin tone look more even over weeks"; "the science on PDRN in skincare is still early".
- **Can't say:** "repairs cells", "rebuilds or boosts collagen", "lifts", "firms", "fades dark spots in 4 weeks", "clinically proven", "same as clinic injections".
- **Can't say:** "exfoliates", "vitamin C", "99% PDRN", "vegan", "hypoallergenic", or quote test numbers like "125% more hydration".
- **Always say:** "Allergic to fish? Ask your doctor first."
