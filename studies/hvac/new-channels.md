## The short answer

- **222 channels in tiers A, B and C started in the last 12 months** (since 2025-10-26): 140 joined YouTube in the window, 73 are older accounts whose first long-form video is in the window (reused accounts), 4 pivoted to heat and cooling, 5 restarted after 12+ months of silence.
- **72 of them are "well above" the median new channel of their tier** (A 29, B 16, C 27; definition below).
- **The biggest risers are not HVAC-repair channels.** Of the top 25, 11 are off-grid / old-ways / free-heat channels, 5 outage and winter-prep channels, 4 HVAC repair channels, 1 brand-verdict channel (HVAC Exposed), 1 frugal-bills channel and 3 mixed. - **Ray Dutton's Garage is copied heavily, and the copies mostly fail.** 115 titles on 51 census channels copy templates Ray started (48 of the channels are new this year); the median copy has 450 views. Only 2 copies passed 100,000 views: The DIY HVAC Guy (an established on-camera channel, 527,000 subs; 1,220,217 views) and Repara Fácil USA (135,069). At least 9 channels copy his look too (a navy work shirt with a name patch, several with "RAY" on it). Ray himself borrowed 3 of his 8 title templates (from HVAC Exposed and How To Home).

## Method

### Who counts as new (the 222)

Tiers A, B and C of the census (791 channels in A-D; D and X are out of scope here: 7 D and 18 X channels are new too, not listed). A channel is new when one of these holds:

| Basis | Rule | Channels |
|---|---|---:|
| joined | YouTube joined date on or after 2025-10-26 (About) | 140 |
| old account, first long-form | joined earlier, but the first long-form video on the Oldest sort is "11mo ago" or newer (an old or bought account reused; reading) | 73 |
| pivot | **my rule:** among the channel's pulled long-form videos, at least 3 before 2025-10-26 with under 10% HVAC titles, and since then at least 5 long-form with at least 3 and 30%+ HVAC titles. Start = the first HVAC long-form in the window. | 4 |
| restart | **my rule:** the newest long-form before the window is "2y ago" or older (12+ months silent) and at least 5 long-form videos since. Start = the first long-form in the window. | 5 |

The pivot and restart tests were only run where the pulled lists cover the window's start (the Latest list is complete, reaches back before the window, or the Oldest list reaches into it); 28 of the 355 older A-C channels could not be tested for this reason (very big back catalogues). "HVAC title" is the census keyword rule (`is_hvac`, 96.3% agreement with the round 2 hand marks), which includes heating, cooling, bills, insulation and outage heat.

The pivots: Why Am I Green (B; 4 of 86 HVAC long-form before the window, 40 of 106 since), Craftsman Chris (B; 0/26 before, 11/13 since: DIY geothermal AC), Eris Life Hub (B; 0/32, 4/12), matty tripps (C; 9/102, 3/6). The restarts: Jordan Dearden (A; last long-form "14y ago", 13 since), Jeremy Shanks (B), iDoThings (B), Rob Here Trev2323 (B), Simple Fix! (A).

### "Well above the niche"

For each new channel, from the census (tab views, rounded):
1. **long-form views since start** (sum of the views of every long-form video since the start);
2. **views of last-90-day uploads** (sum of the views of long-form videos whose age text is certainly under 90 days: up to "2mo ago"; a floor);
3. **subscribers**;
4. **best long-form video since start**.

Each is divided by the median of the new channels in the same tier. **Score** = the geometric mean of the four ratios. **Well above** = score 3 or more **and** at least 2 of the 4 metrics at 3x the tier median or more. Ranking is by score.

Median new channel per tier (the yardstick):

| Tier | New channels | Long-form views since start | Last-90-day uploads' views | Subs | Best video | Long-form videos | Well above |
|:---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 94 | 36,106 | 9,934 | 246 | 16,500 | 19 | 29 |
| B | 56 | 39,801 | 9,114 | 412 | 12,500 | 26 | 16 |
| C | 72 | 277,942 | 62,811 | 2,955 | 137,000 | 43 | 27 |

(The tier C yardstick is about 8x higher: off-grid and outage channels are bigger. A tier A channel needs far fewer views than a tier C one to rank, which is what comparing within the tier is for.)

### The takeoff videos and the first hit (top 25)

- **The 3 videos that took it off** = the 3 most-viewed long-form videos since the start, by exact views (`/next`). Every long-form video since the start with 80,000+ tab views was pulled exactly, so the order is safe.
- **First hit** = the earliest-uploaded long-form video since the start with 100,000+ exact views (my threshold). "Uploads before it" counts in the Videos tab's own order (Latest list, joined to the Oldest list where they overlap), from the first long-form upload since the start: all Videos-tab uploads (5:00+ and shorter) and long-form only. Shorts are not in the Videos tab.
- **Pace** = long-form videos since the start / months since the first long-form upload (exact date).
- Exact views match the tab's rounded views closely (637 videos: median ratio 1.003, all within 10%). Approximate dates were off by up to about 100 days ("1y ago" spans a year), which is why the takeoff dates use exact dates.

### Readings, not measurements

Lane (by rule: the channel's most common lane tag; tier A with none = HVAC repair, or brand verdicts when that is its top sub-niche; B or C with none = mixed), title template (by rule, first match), type and persona (types helper), copycat look (mine, one thumbnail). Each is marked where used.
