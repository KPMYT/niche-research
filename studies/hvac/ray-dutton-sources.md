# Ray Dutton's Garage: where his topics and titles came from (2026-10-26)

Asked by the operator, 2026-10-26: "take ray duttons channel and breakdown where he got his topics from. what his source videos were".

**How:** all 53 of Ray's long-form videos (first upload 2026-07-29) were traced by 4 helpers: his 10 videos with 100K+ views in depth, the other 43 split between two helpers, and a fourth on where his title templates and the "retired pro" persona came from. Then a checker re-tested the main claims and 6 random rows. **Measured:** dates and views (innertube `/next`, pulled 2026-10-26; NexLev's publish dates are off by weeks and were not used), domain registration dates (RDAP), the transcript overlap counts. **Readings:** every match label ("near-copy", "same angle", "template from another niche", "own reuse") and every "where it came from". Multiples ("x") are census multiples: today's views against the channel's 12-month baseline, not the multiple when Ray found it.

Raw data: `data/raw/ray-dutton-videos-2026-10-26.jsonl` (Ray's videos); `data/raw/ray-sources-2026-10-26/` (helper CSVs `ray_sources_h1..h3.csv`, `h4-templates.csv`, `h4-persona-channels.csv`, the checker's `check.csv`, candidate dates, transcripts, sales pages, thumbnail sheets, Ray's descriptions).

## 1. The short answer

1. **His biggest hits are mostly his own title templates, then reruns of them.** 8 of his 10 videos with 100K+ views are a new template or a rerun of one of his own winners. Only 2 (#3 773,793 and #21 140,582) are copies of someone else's video.
2. **When he copies, he copies the #1 outlier of a small new faceless HVAC channel**, mostly **HVAC Exposed** (joined 2026-02-27, 9.59K subs), and re-scripts it in his persona keeping the source's scenes in order. Second source: old big face-channel hits (How To Home, The DIY HVAC Guy, 2022-2025, 1-3M views).
3. **His new templates are proven frames moved in from other niches**, mostly cars ("habits that destroy your car" -> "thermostat habits that wear out your AC years early"). "Hope You Keep Making" existed only on 4 tiny non-trade videos (top: 3,904 views) before him.
4. **The persona itself has a direct predecessor: HVAC Mike** (2.43K subs), same AI tradesman look and e-book pitch, 20 days before Ray. Ray is the polished, far bigger version (6.38M channel views against 168K).
5. **Everyone copies him back now.** Dozens of channels in HVAC, plumbing, cars, boats, RVs (including HVAC Mike and HVAC Exposed), most under 5K views. The big exception is The DIY HVAC Guy's "Before You Turn On Your Heat This Fall, Watch This (Or Pay For It All Winter)" (2026-09-26, 1,220,621). The operator's plumbing channels used his templates from 2026-08-14 on.

## 6. His method, as it reads from the sources

1. **Find the outlier, not the channel:** the #1 video of a small new faceless channel (HVAC Exposed's filter video was 195x) or a years-old 1-3M face-channel hit.
2. **Remake the script, not the words:** keep the source's scenes and comparisons in order, rewrite every line in the persona, add his own ending reveal, an "I'll argue against myself" honesty passage, the raydutton.com plug and a comment prompt.
3. **Make new title shapes by moving proven frames in from other niches**, mostly cars (habits, mistakes, "they don't want you to know"), and add a season ("This Fall", "This Winter", "Before the First Hard Freeze").
4. **Rerun a winner fast:** #31 -> #32 in 3 days, #32 -> #36 in 7, #2 -> #22 in 29. His breakouts are the first use or the first rerun of a new shape on a big evergreen topic (AC shutdown, heat on, thermostat, filter, energy bill).
5. **Lag:** outside sources 1-5 months old (new faceless channels) or 1-10 years (old face-channel hits); his own reruns 3-66 days.
