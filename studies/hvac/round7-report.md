## 2. What the research shows

### The competition (deliverables 1, 2)

- **254 HVAC-first channels for homeowners** (tier A), of which **61 are faceless personas**, 86 faceless and 107 on camera; 81 tier A channels are contractors teaching homeowners. 188 general DIY channels with an HVAC share (B) and 109 in the next-door lanes: off-grid heat, outages, frugal living (C). `channels.md`
- **Ray Dutton's Garage is still the faceless leader:** 31.9K subs, joined 2026-07-01, "Before You Shut Your AC Down This Fall, Do This (Or Pay for It Next Spring)" 1,229,174 views. The two bigger tier A personas are old-ways channels: Eli Yoder Secrets (249K subs, "This $300 Tunnel Keeps Any Home 55°F Forever…" 1.28M) and Earl Knows How (43.9K, "This Cheap Window Trick Keeps Heat Inside All Winter" 545,458).
- **New channels:** 221 started (or pivoted) in the last 12 months; **60 are well above** their tier (with 20%+ of their views on HVAC). Of the top 25, 11 are off-grid / old-ways / free-heat channels, 5 outage and winter-prep, **4 HVAC repair** (Ray Dutton's Garage, JustintimeHVAC, John The HVAC Guy, Exposi), 1 brand verdicts (HVAC Exposed). Takeoff is fast: a median 3 uploads and 7 days to the first 100K video. `new-channels.md`
- **Copying Ray doesn't work for others:** 115 titles on 51 channels copy his templates (median 450 views; 2 past 100K, the big one an on-camera channel, The DIY HVAC Guy, 1,220,217). 9 channels copy his look (navy shirt, name patch, 4 saying "RAY"); none is well above.

## 3. Caveats

1. The multiple compares lifetime views with a recent baseline, so old videos look strong; the tables lead with the last 12 months for that reason.
2. Several rows rest on a few channels (brand verdicts for personas: 57% from 3 channels). The "top 3" column shows this everywhere.
3. Labels are title-only readings. The first emotion is the weakest label (68% on the blind re-label, 77% where both readers gave one).
4. Views on channel tabs are rounded; exact views were pulled for the top 100, recent winners and new-channel takeoff videos.
5. Real vs AI in thumbnails is the weakest thumbnail call (39/45).
6. A logged-in, trained homepage was not studied (it needs the operator's browser).

## 4. Calls taken by default, for the operator to confirm

1. Contractor channels that teach homeowners count as competitors (tier A, marked "contractor").
2. Outage or off-grid channels whose videos are mostly HVAC count as tier A, with their lane noted.
3. DIY stove-building craft channels (cement and barrel stoves) moved from A to the off-grid lane (C), and kept out of the top 100 where they're cooking or craft stoves.
4. Channel types use the brief's four types; "brand" became a separate company column.
5. Four new sub-niche codes used in the labels: scam and upsell, cooling without AC, noises / smells / error codes, general HVAC. Not yet in `labels.md`.
