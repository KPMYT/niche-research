# HVAC study: the top 100 long-form HVAC videos of all time (deliverable 7)

Built 2026-10-26 by `data/tools/top_build.py` (round 3 helper). Exact views and upload dates pulled from YouTube's innertube `/next` endpoint (logged out) on 2026-10-26 (UTC); every row carries its pull time in `top-100.csv` and `data/raw/topvideos-exact-2026-10-26.json`. Lengths are the Videos-tab overlay (census, 2026-10-26) or the search-page length (sweep, 2026-10-24), both to the second. **Not checked yet by a second helper** (round 6).

**What is measured and what is a reading.** Views, upload dates, lengths, baselines and multiples are measured (multiples use the census baseline, which is a median of tab-rounded views). Sub-niche, format, emotions, villain and the in-scope / English decisions are **readings** by the labels.md rules (title first; description only to confirm language). Channel type comes from the round 3 type helper's `data/raw/census-types-*-2026-10-26.csv` where the channel is there, else the census CSV's carried type, else `not found` (the source is named in each cell).

## How the list was built

1. **Pool.** (a) `data/raw/census-videos-2026-10-26.csv`: every long-form (5:00+) video the census pulled from the 1,122 channels of the round 2 master list's tiers A-D (Latest, Popular, Oldest sorts; up to about 120 each). Census channels re-tiered X keep their videos in the pool, so big HVAC videos on off-niche channels count. (b) `data/raw/sweep-videos-2026-10-24.csv`: keyword-sweep videos from any channel. Kept: titles that are HVAC by the census rule (`census_build.hvac_title`) or carry the census `is_hvac` mark. Pool: 41,589 videos (32,444 from the census, 9,145 sweep-only).
2. **Rule pre-filter** (logged): 3,326 titles dropped as kids' challenges, games, vehicles, commercial / industrial / trade training, bushcraft and camping, electronics, non-English titles, and DIY craft stove builds with no heating word. Counts: commercial / industrial / trade training 2,033; craft / cooking stove build, no heating word in the title 580; kids / challenge video 250; electronics / non-HVAC gadget 233; vehicle / RV AC or heat 99; bushcraft / camping shelter 87; not English 37; game 7.
3. **Exact pulls**: 1,543 candidates (the top 600 by approximate views, plus the recent-list candidates) pulled from `/next` (views, date, owner) and oEmbed (original title; logged-out pages auto-translate). 4 of them are no longer in the pool after the round 3 census rule fix (food and drink titles; kept in the raw JSON with the reason): `OlqexwQ0OqU`, `m2UghFhNahU`, `j4Se7moXsj4`, `TVem7Z-r4fY`.
4. **Hand review**: every title that would enter a list was read: 434 kept and 185 dropped by hand across all lists (reasons in `data/tools/top_review.py`). English: the original title by the round 2 rule, plus the description head (another language named, or mostly non-Latin script). Where an English-titled video's audio could not be confirmed, NexLev transcripts were checked for 6 videos (1 Hindi, dropped; 1 English; 1 music only; 3 not available); rows with unconfirmed audio carry a language note in the CSV.
5. **Scope readings used** (main ones, to confirm with the operator): DIY cement / brick / barrel stove builds whose title doesn't mention heating are read as craft or cooking stoves and left out (see the side table below: they would otherwise take most of the top 30); waste-oil 'stoves' that replace cooking gas are out, waste-oil *heaters* are in; bushcraft and camping shelters are out; car AC is out; firewood splitting and stacking are out; insulation, spray foam and weatherstripping are in only when the title is about heating, cooling or the bill (operator rule); utility-bill news stories are out, homeowner bill-cutting videos are in; DIY and passive cooling (homemade coolers, earth tubes) are in, mapped as `not mapped: DIY / passive cooling (proposed sub-niche, not in labels.md)` because labels.md has no cooling equivalent of off-grid heat.
6. **Multiple** = exact views / the channel's baseline (labels.md section 6: median tab views of its long-form uploads in the last 12 months, or of its last 30 if fewer than 10). Census channels: baseline from `census-channels-2026-10-26.csv`. Channels not in the census: pulled for this build with `yt_allsorts.py` (Latest, Popular, Oldest; 4 pages) on 2026-10-26 and computed the same way (287 channels). An old video's multiple is inflated by age; compare multiples with the upload year in view.

The 100th video has 2,757,322 views.

## Problems and limits

- The pool only holds what the census (up to about 120 videos per sort per channel) and the keyword sweep returned; a big HVAC video on a channel neither touched could be missing.
