### 4. Channel pages
For every kept channel, `about_batch.py` loaded the About panel (subscribers, total views, video count, joined date, country) and the Videos tab (latest and Popular). Two About panels failed in the batch and were re-pulled by hand. **On some channels the Popular chip did not load and the tab returned the latest videos instead**, so the "example video" can be a recent video rather than the channel's best.

### 5. Web search: 5 of the 20 allowed
1. best HVAC YouTube channels for homeowners
2. best YouTube channels heating and air conditioning DIY reddit
3. top HVAC YouTubers list 2026
4. YouTube channel lower your energy bill home tips homeowners popular channel
5. "faceless" YouTube channel HVAC retired technician AI voice

These surfaced Anti DIY HVAC, hackfreehvac, HVAC Life, HVAC SIMPLIFIED (AMK), New HVAC Guide (which is HVAC Guide for Homeowners) and Mechanically Inclined. The lists are trade-marketing pages from 2020 to 2024, and they had nothing new on faceless channels for homeowners.

### 6. Check
- **Sample:** 12 random rows (`random.seed(23)`) were loaded again from YouTube.
- **Subscribers and joined date:** matched in all 12. Walt Brandt moved from 461 to 464 subscribers.
- **Example video:** its owner was checked with YouTube oEmbed. It matched in 11 of the 12. Frugal Solutions returned HTTP 401 (embedding is off), so it was not checked.
- **Still to do:** this was a self-check, not the second helper that CLAUDE.md asks for, so a checker should still re-check a sample.

### Columns
- **Subs, total views, joined, videos:** from the YouTube About panel, 2026-10-23. The CSV gives the source for each row. 3 brand-new channels hide their subscriber count, written "not found".
- **NexLev monthly views, monthly revenue, RPM (base) and outlier score:** NexLev's estimates. Only channels that came back in the niche-finder results have them.
- **Example video:** the long-form (5 minutes or more) video with the most views and a heating or cooling word in the title that I saw for the channel in any pull (search, Videos tab or NexLev), with where it was seen. It is not checked to be the channel's all-time top HVAC video.
- **Fit, audience, persona and topics:** my reading from the description and the titles.
- **Joined:** the date the YouTube account was made. Several persona channels sit on old accounts that were taken over for new content, so "joined" understates how new they are:
  - Frank Coyle's Wrench, 2007 (it still has 18-year-old skateboard clips)
  - Shaun Meeker, 2017
  - Gord The Tradesman, 2017
  - Old House Walt, 2008
  - TheCheapFix, 2007

  Two channels still carry the description from an earlier use: Candlelight Archives (a Regency-romance blurb) and Lost Tech Vault ("John The RV Guy").
