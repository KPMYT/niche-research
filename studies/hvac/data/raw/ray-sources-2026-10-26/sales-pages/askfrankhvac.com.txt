Before you buy
Questions homeowners ask me.
I am not handy at all. Is this over my head?
No. Most of the book is about reading paper, not turning wrenches: what the sticker on your unit means, what a line on a quote means, which questions drop the price. The hands-on parts are filter, drain and thermostat level. If a job needs a licensed pro, the book says so and tells you what a fair scope looks like.
Does it cover furnaces too, or only air conditioning?
Both, plus heat pumps and water heaters. The brand, quote, warranty and contractor chapters apply to whatever is in your basement. The seasonal chapters cover heating and cooling separately.
Will it tell me to do my own gas or electrical work?
Never. Gas, combustion, refrigerant and high-voltage wiring are licensed work and the book is explicit about it. What it does is stop you paying $2,000 for the licensed work plus $3,000 of things nobody needed to touch.
How is this different from the free videos?
The videos explain why the industry works the way it does. The book is the reference you open on the night a quote is on your table, organized so you can find the answer in a minute. Six of the 11 chapters were written for the book and never ran on the channel.
Does it tell me what a job should cost in my area?
No, and be careful with anyone who claims to. Prices swing by region, equipment and installer, so a national number is a guess dressed up as a fact. What the book teaches is how to read your own quote, line by line, and see which lines do not belong.
