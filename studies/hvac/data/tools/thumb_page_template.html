HVAC Thumbnail Atlas

Thumbnails from the HVAC study for Roger the HVAC Guy: the all-time top 100 long-form HVAC videos, the recent winners (last 90 days and last 12 months), and the top 5 videos of 57 main channels. Each is filed under one style group. Views and the multiple (views divided by that channel's typical video) are shown under each one. Style labels are readings by eye, not measurements.

Which styles go with the biggest videos

The top-10, top-25 and top-100 columns count the all-time top 100 by views. A style's median views mix all four lists, so compare it within a list using the filter below. The all-time list leans on real photos because most of it is years old; the recent lists lean on AI images.

Sources

Views are exact for the top 100 and recent winners (YouTube's own count, pulled 2026-10-26) and the Videos tab's rounded figure for channel top 5s. The multiple uses each channel's baseline from the census (the median of its long-form videos from the last 12 months, or its last 30); a dead or tiny channel's baseline makes an old hit's multiple huge, so read multiples within a list.
