### 4. Screening (how 2,318 became 164)

1. `r2_candidates.py` merged every result: 2,318 channels not in round 1's lists. It scored each on heating/cooling words in the name, description and recent titles, minus off-topic words (history, cars, PCs, survival fiction). 399 scored 3 or more.
2. I read those 399 plus about 120 lower-scoring ones from the similar-channel lists, and picked 220 (`r2_picks.py`). Left out at this step: non-English channels (Hindi, Urdu, Spanish, French, German, Indonesian), car AC, appliance-only, factory-process, history and survival-fiction channels, power-station and generator reviews (out of scope, operator 2026-10-23), and local contractor channels under 1,000 subscribers.
3. For all 220, round 1's `about_batch.py` loaded the YouTube About panel and the Videos tab (latest and Popular), 2026-10-24.
4. I dropped 56 more after reading their videos (`r2_decide.py`): no long-form heating or cooling videos, all videos under 5 minutes, or the page did not load. Two German channels (Verlorenes Bauwissen, Naturpfad) were dropped because their thumbnails are in German: their English titles are YouTube's automatic translations (my reading).
5. The example video is the most-viewed loaded video of 5 minutes or more with a heating or cooling word in its title (`r2_example.py`). On some channels the Popular tab did not load, so the example can be a recent video rather than the channel's best.
6. Type: I viewed each kept channel's example thumbnail on six contact sheets (30 a sheet) and read the description. A named character drawn as an AI face is a "faceless persona"; an unnamed AI face is an "AI avatar".

Dropped after the page check (56): see the list near the end.

## Problems and notes

1. **NexLev's niche finder rarely holds HVAC channels.** 43 semantic phrasings found only a handful of new HVAC-main channels; similar-channel searches found nearly all of them. The niche finder does hold the AI-persona outage and off-grid channels well.
2. **Faceless flag:** NexLev marks the AI-face personas it holds as `isFaceless: true` with style `ai_avatar`, so the flag alone does not tell a persona from a plain faceless channel; the style field does. 20 of the 36 persona or avatar channels are not in the niche finder, so their type is my thumbnail reading only.
3. **Similar-channel results carry no outlier score or faceless flag,** so "NexLev outlier" is "not found" for most HVAC and trade channels.
4. **NexLev first-video dates are approximate** (all on the 19th to 22nd of a month) and sometimes impossible for older channels (many read "2022-10-19", which looks like a cut-off). They are used only to flag restarts.
5. **Auto-translated titles:** YouTube showed English titles for two German channels (German thumbnails), so other foreign channels may also look English in the data. I dropped the two I spotted.
6. **Popular tab:** on some channels it did not load, so the example may be a recent video, not the channel's best. Ray Holton Secrets' Videos tab did not load at all; its example comes from NexLev's outlier rows ("Why Did We Stop Using Salt To Cool Our Homes?", 217,777 per NexLev) and its thumbnail did not load, so its type is not checked.
7. **Fit, audience, type and persona are my readings.** The HVAC/TRADE/BRAND line is fuzzy for contractor channels that teach homeowners (for example Stephen Shares HVAC Tips at Goettl, Leaky's, PRIME AC AND HEAT).
8. **Big channels that are HVAC-adjacent rather than HVAC-main** were kept with fit ADJ, DIY or OFFGRID (for example Creative inventions LMTN 448,000 subs, desertsun02 265,000, Wonders 181,000, Forgotten American Survival 106,000), so the subscriber-sorted table is led by them, not by HVAC channels.
9. **Web search:** 0 of 10 used.
