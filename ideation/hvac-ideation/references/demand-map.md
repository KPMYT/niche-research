## 3. Mapped (weaker for personas, or footage-bound)

- **Furnaces as a whole** 0.80x (54) — but Ray's heat-season titles (943,591; 246,965; 155,582) show the packaging lifts it: run furnace topics through habits, the season, the cheap part or the brand verdict, never as a plain explainer.
- **Off-grid heat** 1.50x (313) — strong, but led by craft-stove build channels with real footage. Only the knowledge angle (what's safe, what works) fits a faceless channel.
- **Outage heat** 0.97x (162) — on-camera 6.25x; for Roger, the car-runs-the-furnace and family-warm angles (The DIY HVAC Guy 2,450,035 and 4,291,950).
- **Humidity and air quality** 1.02x (37) — winter dry air, humidifier settings, dehumidifiers (Technology Connections 3.30M and 3.40M).
- **Energy bills** 0.85x (107) — weak alone; carry the bill as the stake inside a habit or a draft title.
- **Seasonal maintenance** 0.95x (71) — weak as "maintenance"; works as Ray's "Before You… (Or Pay for It…)" with the cost in the parenthetical.
- **Heat pumps** 0.52x (17), **mini-splits** 0.95x (8) — thin for personas; decision versus (heat pump vs gas furnace) is the way in.
- **Window and portable AC** — tiny persona n, big on camera (window AC 8.31x on camera); cooling-season brand and gadget verdicts.
- **Not a lane:** old-house heat (2 videos past 100k in a year against AC's 72), boilers and radiators (mostly British).
