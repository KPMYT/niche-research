**His method:** (1) take the #1 outlier of a small new faceless channel or a 1-3M face-channel hit; (2) keep the source's scenes in order, rewrite every word; (3) make new title shapes by moving car-channel frames into HVAC and add a season; (4) rerun a winner within 3-7 days. Copies only worked on big topics: his window-AC copy of a 212,738 video did 4,979. **Copying him back doesn't work:** 115 titles on 51 channels copy his templates, median 450 views; 9 channels copy his look, none well above its tier. The one big exception is on camera: The DIY HVAC Guy's "Before You Turn On Your Heat This Fall, Watch This (Or Pay For It All Winter)" 1,220,621.
