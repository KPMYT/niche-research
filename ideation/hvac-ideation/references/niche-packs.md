Settings: basement or utility room, hallway wall, the side yard in snow, a living room in winter light.
- **Text:** white heavy caps with a black outline, 4 words or fewer; yellow #FFEA00 on the one villain or negated word (NOT **BLEACH**); the red command box only for 1-2 word commands (STOP, BEFORE / AFTER).
- **Device:** one red arrow or one red circle, never both; no check or X icons; no magnifier insets (extreme close-ups instead).
- **Season cues:** frost on a window, snow outside, breath-cold blue light outside versus warm light inside — on the stage, never as a filter over the whole frame.
