## 2. HVAC phrases by family

- **Adversary profits from your mistake:** "HVAC Companies Hope You Keep Making" · "Utility Companies Hope You Keep Making" (Ray, 589,530 and 406,059) · the older "Hope You Never Learn".
- **Adversary tags:** "(What HVAC Companies Hide)" · "HVAC Won't Mention" · "Contractors Practically Make Their Living On These" · "Contractors Never Quote Them First" · "Techs Charge $300 to Replace".
- **Insider and lie tags (the trade's own vocabulary as the hook):** "(The Airflow Lie)" · "(The Brand Name Lie)" · "(The Rule-of-Thumb Lie)" · "(The 15-Year Loophole)" · "(The Lifetime List)" · "(The 20-Year List)" · "(The Planned Obsolescence Trap)" · "Blacklist".
- **The season and the cost:** "This Fall" · "This Winter" · "Before the First Cold Snap / Hard Freeze" · "(Or Pay for It Next Spring / All Winter / the First Cold Night / in January)" — Ray's cost parenthetical; the cost is a time the viewer can picture.
- **Lifespan and wear:** "Wear Out Your [AC / Furnace] Years Early" · "Will Last 25 / 30 Years" · "Never Leave You Cold".
- **Expected-answer negation:** "(It's Not the Temperature)" · "(It's Not the Furnace)" · "— And It's NOT Bleach".
- **Brand verdicts:** "The Only [N] [Furnace / AC / HVAC] Brands Worth Buying" · "(Everyone Else Is Overcharging You)" · "Ranked Worst to Best" · "AVOID These" · "STOP Buying This".
- **Price pairs:** "$10 Part" · "$30 Filter" · "$99 Tune-Up" · "$2,500 'Dead Compressor' Quote" · "$400 for a 5 Minute Fix" · "$1,700 Repair" — one cheap number against one tech's number.
- **Sensory symptoms (title in the viewer's words):** feels cold at 70 · cold and clammy · one room never cools · upstairs hot, downstairs cold · keeps shutting off · runs 2 minutes then shuts off · weak airflow · flooding your ceiling · static shocks, dry air.
