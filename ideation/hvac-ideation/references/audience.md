## 1. The viewer, in this niche

The same homeowner who watches Roger the Pipe Guy: an older house, a careful budget, distrust of being upsold, pride in fixing things. In HVAC the fear is a cold house and a big quote, and the distrust has a specific target: the HVAC company's tune-up, the "dead compressor", the replacement it pushes. Mode: browse, timed to the season.

## 3. Money and time language

Rounded prices, one cheap number against one tech's number (`steering.md` §4). The cold night and the after-hours call are the time stakes ("the first cold night", "in January", "2 a.m.").

## 4. Real mechanisms that keep big titles honest

High-MERV filters add airflow resistance on many home systems (and the EPA recommends MERV 13 where the system can take it — fit, not a villain) · condensing furnaces drain water through a trap that clogs · flame sensors coat over and shut the burner off · oversized furnaces short cycle · a single-stage furnace heats at one rate (cranking the thermostat doesn't speed it up) · fan ON runs the blower all day · closed vents raise static pressure · the rim joist and attic hatch leak most of the drafts people feel (ENERGY STAR: about 15% off heating and cooling with air sealing and insulation together) · ceiling fans clockwise on low in winter (DOE, ENERGY STAR) · a 1,500 W space heater draws 12.5 A, above a 15 A circuit's continuous rating · sister brands share factories · R-22 was phased out and R-410A is being phased down.
