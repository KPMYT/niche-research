## Operator decisions every file must respect
- Lead with everyday electrical problems; **fire is the stakes, never the topic** (no fire-prevention topics: space heaters, cords/power strips, "what starts fires", kitchen fires, extinguishers, smoke alarms, dryer vents, lithium battery fires).
- Background mostly unspoken; a retired electrician when needed. Never fire investigator.
- Formats: how-to and "why does X keep doing Y" yes; test/demonstration topics re-made as explainers (explain, never demonstrate); mistakes and "this is dangerous" now and then (crowded); NOT real inspections, real stories, "NEVER leave plugged in" lists.
- Mainly "take and improve on the top vids in our chosen formats from our niche"; borrow viral formats from other persona channels where they fit.
- **Proof rule:** a topic (and a format) counts only with a video of 300K+ views on a faceless channel (AI personas count as faceless) or 1M+ on a real (on-camera) channel; unclear/brand channels get the 1M bar.
- Long-form only (8-17 min). **No money framing** ("we dont really need to use money in electrical"): no dollar anchors in titles or briefs.
- Thumbnails: education = the character beside a blue blueprint explainer board; everything else = the character reacting to or pointing at the problem; may add the character to winning close-ups; thumbnail text about 4 words, commands and questions beat topic labels.
- Villains: the last owner's / handyman's wiring, the cheap product. Never electricians, inspectors or firefighters.
- Channel is **pinned**: Frederick Barnes (AI persona, fictional, channel since 2026-09-21) or Jack Redfield (stopped generator/solar channel, 30-year power-equipment man from rural Tennessee), or both as two test lanes.
