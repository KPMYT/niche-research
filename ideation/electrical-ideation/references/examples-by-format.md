# Examples by format — electrical

Formats with no match in the master keep the study's P-code and wait for an F-number (operator call). Channel-neutral: the channel (Frederick Barnes, Jack Redfield or both) is pinned.

**Proof rule (operator, 2026-10-26):** a format counts only with a video of 300K+ on a faceless channel (AI personas count as faceless) or 1M+ on a real (on-camera) channel; unclear and brand channels get the 1M bar. "Clears" below means it meets that bar.

**Types:** real = on camera; faceless (hands only / diagrams / AI images); AI persona; unclear. Types come from the census (`channels.csv`) or `ideation/channel-types.csv`; "unnarrated" = a hands-only hack channel that a narrated persona can't copy as a format (`top-100.csv` narrated = n).
