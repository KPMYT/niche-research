# Word bank: electrical

The modifier **taxonomy** (parentheticals, urgency, totality, permanence, effort negation, interlock, expected-answer negation, life-stake rung) is in core `format-bank-master.md` §5; the exclusivity ladder in §6. This file holds the electrical **phrases** for each family, the niche vocabulary and the thumbnail words. Caps-word status at profile level is tracked in core `audience-profiles.md` (Profile A).

**Sources and the bar.** Every figure is from `studies/electrical/`, file named. "In-lane titles" = the 2,878 long-form titles in `data/merged/videos-with-multiples.csv` outside the fire, money, inspection and holiday-light sub-niches (counted for this file, 2026-10-27). ✓ = at least one title clears the operator's proof bar (300K+ on a faceless or AI-persona channel, 1M+ on a real or unclear channel; `CONCLUSIONS.md`, `log.md` "Operator rule for topics"). Channel types from `channels.csv` / `ideation/channel-types.csv`. Word-type labels are readings.

**Format codes.** F-numbers are the master's. Electrical-only formats keep the study's P-code (`ideation/proven-formats.csv`). Each P-code is a candidate awaiting an F-number (operator call).

## 3. Insider and villain wording

- **Exclusivity ladder (niche wording):** "Most DIYers Don't Know" → "99% of People ... WRONG" → "Why Don't People Know This About" → "The Difference Nobody Explains". Stop there. The next rung ("[Insiders] don't want you to know") makes electricians the adversary, which the villain rule forbids.
- **Quiet insider, no job title:** "after all the houses I've worked in" (`CONCLUSIONS.md` §7; script voice, not title evidence) · own "I Find in Almost Every Older House" A29 · thumbnail "PROS KNOW THIS" (580K ✓). "7 Things I'll Never Do As An Electrician" (Backyard Maine, 1.41M ✓) proves the job title works, but the background stays mostly unspoken: "retired electrician" only when needed.
- **Villains (lightly; 70-80% of videos have none):** the cheap product ("Why Cheap LED Lights Keep Glowing", 3.76M ✓; "I tested 7 Wago Knock-Offs", GreatScott!, 377K) · the last owner's or handyman's wiring (title evidence thin: 4 titles name a previous owner or handyman, top 74K; carry it in the brief and the picture) · Federal Pacific / Zinsco as a villain inside a video (50 titles, typical 4,747, none over 1M; not a topic).

## 6. Words to avoid

- **Fire as the topic.** FIRE, BURN, "starts fires", MELTING as the subject or the main thumbnail word. In-lane titles with "fire": 56, typical 851, none over 1M; burnt / melted 14, typical 1,045. Fire-first topics are out by decision ("we dont want any more fire type vids for now"). Fire and shock come in only as the stakes, in a tail.
- **"Electricians HATE This" and every electrician-, inspector- or firefighter-as-adversary line.** Such titles got 31K and 375 views (`CONCLUSIONS.md` §4). Viewers treat these people as the trusted insiders.
- **Money words.** SAVE, $[N], THOUSANDS, Hundreds, bill, CUT, "costs". Out by the 2026-10-24 decision ("we dont really need to use money in electrical"), even where proven ("SAVE $300 EACH!!", HandyDadTV, 1.45M; "Save Thousands!", LRN2DIY, 1.01M). Money worry was almost absent from the titles anyway (4 of 262, `CONCLUSIONS.md` §4).
- **"Buried / banned by the industry" framing.** Jack Redfield's last six uploads used it ("Why Did Honda Bury It?", "What Dealers Don't Want You Buying") and got 87-501 views (`data/jack-redfield/videos.csv`).
- **NEVER-list openers** ("[N] Things You Should NEVER Leave Plugged In"): 143 videos, typical 225; the format is out by decision.
- **Filmed-test words.** TESTED, "I Tested", Experiment, "For Science!", "Watch What Happens". We explain, never demonstrate. Silver Cymbal's "Wire Nut VS Wago TESTED" (768K) is remade without "TESTED". This is a reading of the operator's rule.
- **Persona job titles in the title or thumbnail.** Firefighter, fire investigator, inspector. "Retired electrician" only when needed.
- **"SUICIDE CORD".** Silver Cymbal's 11.6M short uses it; our B11 thumbnail chose KILLER CORD. Reading: keep the softer word. Operator call.
