## 2. Standing decisions (operator, quoted in `studies/electrical/log.md`)

1. **Everyday electrical problems lead; fire is the stakes, never the topic** ("yes fire can be stakes agreed"; "we dont want any more fire type vids for now").
2. **Formats:** how-to ("how to is good"); "why does X keep doing Y" ("is good"); the test and demonstration topics re-made as explainers ("we cannot do exact demonstrations... WE STILL TAKE THE BEST TOPICS"); mistakes and "this is dangerous" now and then ("work but obviuosly high competition").
3. **Not doing:** real inspections, real stories ("wont work"), "NEVER leave plugged in" lists ("we dont really want to proceed for now"), the fire investigator ("not rly education. not our current lane").
4. **Source of ideas:** "we mainly though want to take and improve on the top vids in our chosen formats from our niche", plus viral formats from other personas where they fit.
5. **Proof rule** (2026-10-26): "topics with low views ARE NOT GOOD. we need to be seeing at least 300k if its from a faceless channel, 1m views if its from a real channel". AI personas count as faceless; unclear and brand channels get the 1M bar. The same bar applies to formats.
6. **Long-form only** ("yes long form only for sure"), 8-17 minutes, typically 11 (in-lane videos, `CONCLUSIONS.md` §3).
7. **No money framing** ("we dont really need to use money in electrical", 2026-10-24): no dollar anchors in titles, thumbnails or briefs.
8. **Thumbnails:** the character with a blue blueprint explainer board for education; otherwise a person reacting to or pointing at the problem; the character may be added to winning close-ups. Track thumbnail text on recent winners (`niche-packs.md`).
9. **Silver Cymbal's 1M+ electrical videos** may be re-made with lightly altered titles ("the silver cymbal can be a copy paste of his most popular ones... we just alter title slightly"), as long-form versions, explained rather than demonstrated.

## 3. Base ideation plan

- **Batch build** (the method that produced the 17 initial ideas, 2026-10-26):
  1. Pick proven topics (`demand-map.md` §1), hottest first, skipping topics the produced log already covers unless the angle is new.
  2. For each, take the biggest proven video on the topic in a chosen format and improve its title (clearer why, the stakes, the villain in the walls); or put the topic into a proven format from `examples-by-format.md`.
  3. Show each idea with its reference video and thumbnail, the reference's posting date, the topic's heat (uploads since on that channel and others, last-12-months typical) and whether it clears the proof bar. The operator asked for this layout ("I like how you are pitching the ideas to me with reference vid a thumb").
  4. The operator picks by number; then thumbnail instructions (simple, with reference links) and briefs (core brief spec plus this niche's rules in `SKILL.md` §3).
- **Mix:** core initial-batch mix (80-85% proven topics, 15-20% new) until the channel has its own data. The first batch was all proven topics.
- **Per batch:** how-to and "why does X" carry most titles; explainers for the test topics; at most a few mistakes or danger titles; generator safety only on the lane that takes it (Jack's, if the operator chooses it).
- **Near-twins** (same topic, different angle: A2/C2, A12/C12, A31/B11 in the first batch) are allowed; each needs a different thumbnail picture.

## 5. Topic boundaries

- **In:** everything in `demand-map.md`: outlets and switches, ceiling fans, lighting and LED, wire connectors, GFCI and AFCI, old wiring, testers, EV chargers and 240 V outlets, breakers and panels, generator connection safety, wiring mistakes, electrical explainers.
- **Out (operator calls):** every fire-first topic (space heaters, cords and power strips, "what starts fires", kitchen fires, extinguishers, smoke alarms, dryer vents, lithium battery fires); the electric bill (and money in general); home inspection walkthroughs; holiday lights; generator buying guides and product reviews; solar and small engines unless the operator opens Jack's old lane.
- **Villains only, not topics:** Federal Pacific and Zinsco panels (3.7K typical, none over 1M), the last owner's or handyman's wiring, cheap knock-off products. Never electricians, inspectors or firefighters ("Electricians HATE This" titles got 31K and 375 views).
- **Generators:** connection safety (backfeed, interlocks, transfer switches, inlets) is in; whether it becomes a strand of the channel or waits for its own channel is open (`CONCLUSIONS.md` §9).
