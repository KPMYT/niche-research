# Niche packs: electrical (for the `thumbnails` skill)

The `thumbnails` skill loads the named channel's pack as standing context (its rule hierarchy level 2: user/niche context beats defaults). Fields: character description(s), common objects / materials / settings, banned items, style tweaks. The installed skill splits a niche into a niche file (`niches/<niche>.md`: channels, status, niche notes, hard rules) and one channel file per channel (`channels/<name>.md`: niche, presence, status, pack). §1 below is the niche file. §2 holds the two candidate channel packs.

**Status: DRAFT, `endorsed`-pending.** The two looks, the text rule and the 17 thumbnail instructions are the operator's own decisions (2026-10-26 / 2026-10-27). Everything else is drafted from the study. **The channel is pinned** (Frederick Barnes, Jack Redfield, or both as two test lanes), and the operator has still to fix the character's look. Until then the skill writes "the character" and says so in the label line. It never invents a face.

### Banned items

- **The dark AI night scene with something burning as a default.** It is the look of most small persona channels, whose typical top video is about 5K. Frederick's one hit (439K) used it; that is the exception (`CONCLUSIONS.md` §5).
- **Flames as the subject.** A small light brown scorch is the most fire shown (A12, A29).
- **Fire-first props as the subject:** space heaters, power strips, smoke alarms, dryer vents, lithium batteries.
- **Other channels' logos.** Remove the source's logo when recreating (A1, A2, A12).
- **Brand names and logos unless the topic needs them.** Reading: "Wago" is the plain word for lever connectors and appears when the video is about them (Wire Nut vs Wago); otherwise show a generic connector.
- **Demonstrations that look filmed.** No test rigs, no sparks from a test, no burning insulation, no live meter readings framed as our test. Show the result as a diagram or a still of the part ("we cannot do exact demonstrations").
- **Money.** No dollar figures or prices as text (decision 2026-10-24). A plain price tag on a store hook is fine as a prop (B11, approved).
- **The wrong practice shown as the recommendation.** It appears only when marked wrong: a red X, a red circle or the character's stop hand (A31, C2, A29).
- **Electricians, inspectors or firefighters as the villain**, and any job-title badge, patch or uniform on our character.

### Niche hard rules (bind the image, the text and the concept)

1. Everyday electrical problems lead. Fire and shock are the stakes, never the topic.
2. Explain, never demonstrate. Test topics are remade as explainers.
3. No money framing.
4. Villains: the last owner's or handyman's wiring, the cheap product. Never electricians, inspectors or firefighters.
5. The background stays mostly unspoken; "retired electrician" only when needed. Never a fire investigator.
