## 5. Lessons and head-to-heads

1. **Never make the electrician the villain.** "Electricians HATE This One Trick / Save Thousands on Fake Fuse Board Replacements" 31,144 (ToolBox Talk For Electricians) and "5 Electrical Mistakes Your Electrician Hopes You Never Notice" 375 (Mr. Voltage, AI persona; Roy Dalton Electric's near-twin got about 12K, `channels.md`) against the same "hope you keep making" shape aimed at utility companies, 405,809 (Ray Dutton's Garage) [SW, RW]. The counter-case, "Electricians Hate Me for Showing You This!" 1,367,283 (Fix This Build That, real, 1y ago [SW]), is a real on-camera channel with its own audience; the operator's villain rule stands.
2. **Copying a winner's layout is not enough.** How To Home's "7 FATAL Wiring Mistakes..." got 12.2M with a real man pointing at a wire nut and a Wago ("Wrong! Bad!"). Roy Dalton Electric's AI persona copied the exact layout and got 16,643 (`thumbnails/report-channel-top5.md`). Improve the original (a clearer why, the stakes, the villain in the walls, our own thumbnail look), don't trace it (`CONCLUSIONS.md` §7).
3. **The channel's base matters on the same topic.** Jack Redfield's "Transfer Switch vs. Interlock Kit: Which is BEST for Your Generator?" got 5,100 [JR]; How To Home's "How To Choose Between A Transfer Switch Vs Interlock Kit With Power Inlet Box" has 966,567 (real, 5y ago [SW]). Same topic and shape; the difference is the channel's years of audience (reading).
4. **Thumbnail text: commands and questions beat labels.** In lane (49 videos, small samples), commands typically got 531K ("This Needs To STOP!", "START HERE!") and questions 520K ("WHICH ONE KILLS YOU?", "Why 120V?"), against 211K for plain topic labels. Typical length: 4 words. Repeating the title or adding to it made no clear difference (`log.md`, thumbnail text study).
5. **The explainer-board look is open.** No thumbnail in the 403 labelled pairs shows a presenter beside a blueprint board. Nearest: The Wiring Guide's real man beside drawn live/neutral/ground paths, "EXPLAINED", 1.04M (63K for his next), and a plain blueprint "WHICH ONE KILLS YOU?", 841K (The Apprentice's Guide).
6. **Winning explainer titles get copied within weeks.** Modern-Day Edison's "What Does the Neutral Wire Actually Do" (2026-08-03, 520K, 925 times its typical video) was followed by Weirdward Bound's same title (2026-10-14, 272K) [RW]. Check `ideation/topic-heat.csv` for uploads since before re-making one.
7. **NEVER lists: big exceptions, tiny typical.** Home Hazard 698K and Frederick 439K are outliers; the format's typical is 225 across 143 videos. Out by operator call.
8. **The format's typical tells you the risk.** How-to: typical 38K (1,478 videos). Problem-fix: 22K (73 videos, the least crowded). Mistakes: 1.6K (781). Danger warning: 781 (47) (`formats.md`). Length doesn't decide winners: 100K+ videos run the same 8-17 minutes as the rest (`CONCLUSIONS.md` §3).
